In plain terms

What a P-Shot
actually is

PRP stands for platelet rich plasma. A small amount of your blood is taken, spun in a centrifuge to separate it, and the concentrated part is injected into specific areas.

The material is your own plasma, with no synthetic filler.

Restart Medical offers the P-Shot as one comprehensive treatment.

Results vary between people and are not guaranteed. A consultation is used to work out whether the treatment is suitable for you before anything is booked.

04 / The treatment

A little context,
before you talk.

Platelet-rich plasma, or PRP, is prepared from a small sample of your blood. The plasma is then injected into the treatment area.

Restart Medical offers the P-Shot as a single comprehensive treatment. It uses your own plasma, with no synthetic filler.

Results differ from person to person. No outcome is guaranteed.

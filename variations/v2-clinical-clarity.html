The material

Platelet-rich
plasma. Explained.

PRP is the concentrated part of a blood sample. For the P-Shot, the clinic prepares it from your blood and injects it into the treatment area.

The treatment uses your own plasma rather than synthetic filler. It is offered here as one comprehensive treatment.

Read the preparation detail

A small blood sample is processed in a centrifuge to separate the plasma. Ask Alan to explain how the preparation and treatment plan relate to your consultation.

Suitability

This may not be
the right option for you.

Suitability is a clinical decision, not something this page can establish.

Tell Alan about your health, medicines and previous reactions. The paper specifically flags clotting problems and blood-thinning medication for assessment. Do not change medication on the strength of this page.

If you need a guaranteed result, this treatment cannot offer one.
